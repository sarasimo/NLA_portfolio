## A mid summer night dream
A scene from Shakespeare's a midsummer night's dream. After Hermia elopes with Lysander her betrothed Dimitrius sets out to find her. In this scene Demitruis is accosted by Helena in the woods. Helena is hopelessly enamoured with Dimitrius and begs for a scrap of his affection. Oberon, the king of the Fairies sees this exchange and decides to intervene on her behalf.
